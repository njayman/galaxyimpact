# Progression: achievement points and the skill tree

**Status:** Idea, 2026-10-22. Not built.

- **Achievements give points, not unlocks.** Every achievement is worth exactly 1 point. Nothing unlocks directly from an achievement any more.
- **Skill tree.** 1 point unlocks 1 node; nodes have no other cost. Unlocking a node opens the paths leading on from it.
- **Ships.** Only the Ranger is available at the start. Other ships are nodes in the tree: the player navigates the tree to reach and unlock them.
- **Signatures.** Unlocking a ship also unlocks that ship's signature.

## Open

- Tree shape: one shared tree or a branch per ship; how many nodes; what the non-ship nodes grant (weapons, skills, passives).
- What "signature" covers for each ship (signature weapon, Nerve burst, or both).
- Migration: what existing saves keep (ships and weapons already unlocked through achievements).
- Can points be refunded or the tree reset.
